Offline maps

Maps default to Offline/local, including older installations that previously
loaded an online provider without a recorded privacy choice. The included world
overview works without a network connection; it shows country outlines, not
streets. Geographic names and disputed boundaries follow the source dataset.

Settings > Advanced > Map provider selects Offline/local, OpenStreetMap,
Esri satellite or Tianditu. Selecting an online service enables requests to that
service, disclosing your IP address and the viewed geographic area. A missing
tile never silently selects a different provider. Tianditu requires your token.

For detailed offline coverage, choose a local raster .mbtiles map file under
the existing provider control. PNG, JPEG and WebP raster tiles are supported;
vector/PBF packs are not. Detailed local packs currently require Windows and a
local volume: network paths, mapped network drives and reparse points are
rejected before reading the pack. The bundled overview works on all platforms.
Map files are opened read-only. Missing tiles reveal
the bundled overview underneath and never cause an online download. The pack's
attribution is displayed as text. Obtain packs under their provider's terms;
do not bulk-download OpenStreetMap's public tile service for offline use.
