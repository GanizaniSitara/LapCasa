Optional FFmpeg video tools

The public Windows ZIP does not contain FFmpeg or ffprobe. They are separate
programs with their own terms; obtain them directly from a provider if needed.
The app does not automatically download or install these programs.
Official project/download information: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
Windows build provider: https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/

After reviewing your chosen provider's terms, extract the tools yourself.
Create an ffmpeg folder beside lap-casa.exe and place the binaries there as:
  ffmpeg/ffmpeg-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc.exe
  ffmpeg/ffprobe-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc.exe
These are the app's expected names; rename the extracted ffmpeg.exe and
ffprobe.exe accordingly. Keep other required files from your chosen provider
if using a shared build. Restart LapCasa after changing the tools.

Without these tools, video processing and FFmpeg-backed image formats are
unavailable; native supported photo browsing/search/editing remain available.
Not every codec/format/provider version has been tested.
Collected FFmpeg notices in third-party are reference/build provenance;
they do not mean FFmpeg binaries are shipped in this public package.
